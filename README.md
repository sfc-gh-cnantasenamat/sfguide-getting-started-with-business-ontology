# Getting Started with Business Ontology

A Snowflake developer guide by Tianxia Jia. Build a governed Business Ontology on top of three well-built semantic views, connect it to a Cortex Agent, and measure what it adds with a fair A/B test on a synthetic SAP supply-chain dataset.

- Guide: [`quickstart/getting-started-with-business-ontology/getting-started-with-business-ontology.md`](quickstart/getting-started-with-business-ontology/getting-started-with-business-ontology.md)
- Companion files: [getting-started-with-business-ontology](https://github.com/sfc-gh-cnantasenamat/getting-started-with-business-ontology), with the setup scripts in `sql/` and `sql/v2/`, the benchmark harness in `benchmark/`, and the comparison web app in `web/`.

> **Private Preview:** Business Ontology must be enabled on your Snowflake account before use.
